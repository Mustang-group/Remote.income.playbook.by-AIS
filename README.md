## 🎯 Overview

**রিমোট ইনকাম ব্লুপ্রিন্ট** is a practical, anti-scam, execution-focused handbook built specifically for the socio-economic and technological ground reality of Bangladesh. 

Unlike conventional training centers selling outdated courses (Data Entry, basic SEO, form filling), this playbook provides an immediate, verifiable **Proof-of-Work (PoW)** system designed to help university students, unemployed youth, and 9-to-5 moonlighters secure foreign retainers ($150–$450/month) using only a smartphone and free AI tools.

---

## ⚡ Key Highlights & Architecture

- **Interactive Decision Engine:** Integrated 3-step recommendation filter (Time, English proficiency, and skill affinity) that dynamically calculates projected earnings and matches free training tracks.
- **Mobile-First Production Playbooks:** Specific technical rules for CapCut (bouncy subtitles, 0.3s silence trim, watermark removal) and Canva (The 3-Element High-CTR Rule).
- **Zero-Friction Client Delivery:** Replaces bulky Google Drive links with unlisted YouTube Shorts streaming links for instant 1-tap playback on mobile.
- **Spam-Proof Outreach Engine:** Solves the free `@gmail.com` spam-trap problem with native platform outreach (X DMs, LinkedIn Connection Notes, Instagram DMs), follow-up scripts, and 3-tier pricing menus.
- **Bangladesh Bank & NBR Compliance:** Clear protocols on FE Circular 22 ($20,000 threshold), the strict Name-Match AML rule, Proceeds Realization Certificates (PRC), Zero-Tax Return filing, and the **$150 Threshold Rule** (bKash vs. Direct Bank Wire with 2.5% incentive).
